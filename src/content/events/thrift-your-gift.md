Find one-of-a-kind holiday gifts and decorations while giving rescued animals a second chance. Every dollar raised goes to the care of the animals at **Willing Spirit Animal Sanctuary**.

## Donate items

We would love any donations to help put this event on!

We're collecting **clean, gently used, giftable items**, including:

- Books
- Holiday decorations
- Home décor and household goods
- Accessories, jewelry, and handbags
- New or like-new toys and games

Please, no large furniture, damaged items, mattresses, or clothing that's stained or worn out. [Contact us](/contact/) to arrange a drop-off.

## Volunteer

We need help collecting, sorting, pricing, setting up, selling, and cleaning up. Visit [Get Involved](/get-involved/#volunteer) to sign up.

## Can't make it?

Tell a friend, share this page, or [send supplies](/get-involved/#supplies) for our animals.
